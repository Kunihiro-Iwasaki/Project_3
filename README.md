# Project3

## Title
### 78 Illegal Basements in Flood-Prone Areas of New York —Five Years After Hurricane Ida, Which Claimed 11 Lives
## Short description of what aimed to accomplish
#### Do you remember Hurricane Ida five years ago? Eleven people living in basement apartments died in floods caused by torrential rain. They were trapped when water poured in and were unable to escape in time. With the housing market in New York tight, there is still no shortage of people living in basement apartments, where rents are relatively low. As the risk of torrential rain increases due to climate change, I examined the disaster risks associated with basement apartments.
## Short description of findings
#### I overlaid New York City’s flood hazard map onto the 1,653 buildings with illegal basement apartments that the city identified as violations following Hurricane Ida in September 2021. The results revealed that 78 of these buildings are located in flood-prone areas. I also found that this number would rise to 103 at 2050 sea-level projections and to 547 at 2080 sea-level projections. This highlights how climate change is significantly increasing disaster risks.
